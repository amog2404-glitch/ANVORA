# Design System: ANVORA — Autonomous Intelligence
## Premium Apple-Inspired Liquid Glass UI Architecture

**Descriptor:** AUTONOMOUS INTELLIGENCE  
**Tagline:** Connected Intelligence. Autonomous Action.

---

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
ANVORA employs a signature **Translucent Liquid Glass** aesthetic inspired by modern macOS/visionOS spatial computing and high-end vitrics. Rather than flat, generic dark panels, ANVORA creates depth through multi-layer glass transmission:

1. **Ambient Caustic Light Field:** Five distinct chromatic caustic light sources positioned behind the frosted glass pane create organic, flowing illumination with chromatic aberration and refraction.
2. **Frosted Glass Panel:** High-blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(190%)`), subtle frosted white tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`), and sharp inner bevel highlights (`box-shadow: inset 1.5px 0 2px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`).
3. **Physical 3D Glass Sphere Assembly:** The centerpiece voice controller is rendered as a multi-tier physical glass orb with specular apex highlights, vitreous lens reflection, luminous violet core, and fluid ripples.
4. **Floating Frosted Acrylic Dock:** The chat input dock floats above the bottom pane with internal specular edge gleam.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Base & Spatial Environment
- **Canvas Gradient:** Radial gradient from `#FAF9FE` to `#EDEAF6`.
- **Primary Caustic Violet (`#7772E8`)**: Core brand luminosity and focused state glows.
- **Secondary Indigo (`#6366F1`)**: Lower caustic accent and speech cadence illumination.
- **Tertiary Soft Lavender (`#C4B5FD`)**: Ambient atmospheric wash.

### Content & Contrast
- **Text Primary (`#1D1D26`)**: Deep ink black for high legibility (exceeds WCAG AAA requirements).
- **Text Secondary (`#777784`)**: Refined neutral gray for timestamps, secondary labels, and metadata.
- **Glass Border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`)**: Beveled edge highlights simulating physical glass refraction.

---

## 3. Typography Rules

### Brand Typography
- **Header Brand (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 800 weight, uppercase, tracking-wider)**: Used for the authoritative **ANVORA** wordmark.
- **Descriptor Pill (`Inter`, 700 weight, 9px, uppercase, tracking-widest)**: "AUTONOMOUS INTELLIGENCE".

### Interface Typography
- **Primary Interface (`Inter`, sans-serif)**: Clean, geometric grotesk with tabular numerals for activity timers and latency counters.
- **Font Sizes**: Normal (12px-13px), Large (14px-15px), Extra Large (16px) accessible via Settings.
